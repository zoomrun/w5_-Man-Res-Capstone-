# Hangar Teach Capstone Materials

This repository contains reference materials for coaches and leaders supporting a Hangar Teach capstone. The materials show how squads turn an evidence-backed opportunity into a small, testable prototype and a clear next decision.

Manage Reservation (ManRes) is used throughout as an illustrative case. Its opportunities, measures, dates, and product details are not a prescribed capstone scope for other teams.

## Coach And Leadership Focus

- Keep the team anchored in a specific user problem and evidence, not a preselected solution.
- Ask teams to label evidence, assumptions, and unknowns clearly.
- Protect the brief checkpoint after each teach session; it should result in one decision or reduced uncertainty.
- Evaluate the capstone as a learning and decision-making exercise, not as a production release.
- During demos, ask what the prototype proved, what remains risky, and what decision is needed next.
